Where jobs slip away

  1. Missed calls

    Many people who reach voicemail don’t leave a message. They just call the next business on the list.

  2. Slow replies

    A website form or message that waits until evening can lose to a competitor who answered in minutes.

  3. Forgotten estimates

    Plenty of quotes aren’t turned down. They’re forgotten, and the job goes to whoever checked in.

  4. Hard-to-use forms

    If asking for a quote feels slow or confusing, customers give up before you ever hear from them.

What changes

  • Every customer hears back

    Callers and messages get a timely answer, even when you’re on a job, so fewer go elsewhere.

  • More quotes turn into a yes

    Open estimates get a friendly follow-up at the right time instead of going quiet.

  • Less chasing for you

    You spend your day on the work, not keeping track of who still needs a callback.

  • You can see the difference

    You’ll know how many inquiries came in, how fast they were answered and how many became jobs.
