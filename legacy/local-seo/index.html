Where local search loses you calls

  1. Missing from the map

    People searching nearby choose from the map. If you’re not there, they never know you exist.

  2. Out-of-date listing

    Wrong hours or a missing phone number send calls to a competitor. At $400 a job, one lost call a week is about $20,000 a year.

  3. Few or old reviews

    Customers compare reviews before they call. A thin record makes the choice for them.

  4. A website that doesn’t back you up

    Google looks at your site too. If it doesn’t say what you do and where, you’re harder to find.
