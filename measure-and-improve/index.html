The work

  • Tracking

    We track where calls, forms and messages come from: your Google listing, website, ads and referrals. That means tracking numbers where they make sense, and asking every new customer how they heard about you.

  • Monthly report

    One page each month in plain words: what we did, inquiries and booked jobs by source, what each cost where the data allows, and what comes next. Jobs and revenue are the numbers that count; clicks and views are background.

  • Automation

    When a task repeats every week, such as a review request, a reminder or a follow-up, we automate it so it happens without anyone having to remember.

  • Your accounts, your data

    Search Console, Google Business Profile insights and website analytics are set up in your own accounts, so the numbers stay yours.
