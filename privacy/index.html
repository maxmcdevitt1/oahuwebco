Privacy policy

How we handle information you share through our website.

Effective October 14, 2026

Information you send us

When you request a free review through our contact form, we collect the name, business name, email address or phone number, and any message you choose to send. If you contact us directly by email, we receive the information you include in that message.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal or financial information through the contact form.

How we use your information

We use what you send to respond to inquiries, discuss requested services, and keep normal business correspondence and records. We do not sell contact information submitted through the form.

Contact form and email

Our contact form uses FormSubmit to deliver submissions to our business email. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. Messages delivered to our email account may be kept longer for correspondence, business records, or legal obligations.

Website hosting and fonts

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Our typefaces are served from our own website, so loading them does not connect your browser to a third party.

As of the effective date, this website does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing-tracking cookies.

Other websites

We link to services such as Instagram and Facebook. If you follow those links, their privacy practices apply to your activity there.

Keeping information and your choices

We keep correspondence as long as reasonably necessary to handle an inquiry, maintain business records, or meet legal requirements. You can ask us about information you have sent, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

For privacy questions or requests, email us at max@oahuwebco.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The effective date at the top will show when it was last updated.
