# Oahu Web Co. — website sales-copy rules

Approved October 13, 2026. Apply to all future public-facing website edits.

1. Sell the problem and its business cost, not the implementation. Show a recognizable missed opportunity and position Oahu Web Co. as the solution.
2. Keep copy short and direct, written in the owner's language. Prefer one concise service description or FAQ answer.
3. Never reveal or enumerate exact tactics, configurations, software tools, or step-by-step delivery methods in public sales copy unless expressly requested.
4. Never add subtext: no second supporting blurb, decorative caption, subtitle, or explanatory line below headings, hero, cards, images, buttons, or calls to action.
5. Do not add filler or repeat the sales process and pricing across every service page. Preserve the established navy-and-cream design and the existing core site structure.
6. Do not fabricate proof or guarantee rankings, calls, jobs, or revenue. State ownership accurately: the client's accounts and completed work remain theirs, but vendor subscriptions may end with cancellation.
7. This rule applies to promotional copy only. Technical details and legally required disclosures belong in the private proposal, agreement, or applicable policy. Do not hide material limitations.

Keep place-name spelling correct (Oʻahu, Kāneʻohe), the tagline "The online side, handled.", and the business's "we" voice.
